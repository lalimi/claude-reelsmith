# Hook types (evidence: docs/retention.md)

Rule for every hook: the first spoken line, 3–5 words of on-screen text and the first frame say the same thing, and it works on mute. Topic clear by 4 s, no jargon in line one. Never put a code-word CTA inside the hook.

| # | type | evidence | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | context → "but" → snapback | Kallaway [expert] | everyone thinks a course sells expertise. but that's the least important part. people pay for one sentence on the page |
| 2 | six words: who + action + non-obvious result | Kallaway [expert] | I opened every shop that made a sale in September and found the same tiny thing in each |
| 3 | negative / contrarian | each negative word +2.3% CTR across 22,743 headline A/B tests (Nature Human Behaviour 2023) | stop giving away free guides, they teach your audience to never buy from you |
| 4 | "you" + pain as a condition | Kallaway [expert] | if your course has been sitting in your notes for six months, it's not perfectionism, you're scared to name the price |
| 5 | foreshadow the payoff | only type with both reach +55% and engagement +50% (Fanpage Karma 2023, 700 reels, weak) | I gave Claude Code my shop for seven days, and on day five it did what I hadn't dared to do for a year |
| 6 | POV / "when…" | beat question hooks 3–6× on TikTok (vendor data 2026) | when you sell your first guide and your mum is already asking if you can quit your job |
| 7 | question | contradictory evidence (+160% IG reach 2023 vs weakest on TikTok 2026): test it as a trial reel | how much does a guide author with 2,000 followers actually earn? |
| 8 | in medias res, picture first | 63% of top-CTR TikTok ads show the key message in 3 s | a terminal error on screen + "this is when Claude fixed in a minute what I was billed for" |
| 9 | borrowed famous story | Kallaway [expert] | Duolingo keeps people for years with one fear of an owl. your course can do the same |

Emotion: negativity stops the scroll (clicks), but sends come from humour, awe, usefulness and identity (Berger & Milkman 2012; sadness lowers sharing).
