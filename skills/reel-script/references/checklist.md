# Checklist (run before showing any script)

- [ ] **Hook in the first sentence** stops the scroll: contrarian, stakes, absurd humor or confession. A stranger who has never heard of the product would keep watching.
- [ ] **Starts broad, narrows** to the niche no earlier than ~40% of the runtime (structures A, C, D). The product appears as the creator's story, not as an ad.
- [ ] **Three concrete examples or scenes**, escalating, with real-life detail (time, place, object, number).
- [ ] **A named character or a coined term** at least once.
- [ ] **Flowing sentences**: no run of 2+ sentences of one to three words; any short line is a punchline after a setup.
- [ ] **One idea per reel**; every sentence moves the story.
- [ ] **Ending matches the structure** (code word from the hook / cliffhanger + teaser / CTA as the last tip / empowering choice) and there is exactly one CTA.
- [ ] **Length**: 40–50 s ideal, 60 s max, timed at delivery pace (or from the first TTS take).
- [ ] **Facts**: every product claim is in `facts.md`; everything else is listed at the end under "Confirm:".
- [ ] **No copied lines** from reference reels.
- [ ] **Language**: natural in the target language (no calques, nothing that sounds translated); names, prices and idioms localised.
- [ ] **On-screen text**: no em-dashes; accent words marked with `<em>`; numbers in digits.
- [ ] **Montage plan**: 3–5 techniques, each tied to a beat.
